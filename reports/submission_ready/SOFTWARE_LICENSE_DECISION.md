## Common options to consider

| Licence | Practical effect | Typical reason to choose | Key consideration |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| MIT | Permissive reuse with attribution and warranty disclaimer | Maximum simplicity and broad academic/industrial reuse | Does not include an explicit patent grant comparable to Apache-2.0 |
| Apache-2.0 | Permissive reuse plus explicit patent licence/termination provisions | Broad reuse where patent language is desirable | Longer/more detailed obligations than MIT |
| GPL-3.0 | Strong copyleft for distributed derivative works | Intention to keep redistributed derivatives under GPL-compatible terms | May be unsuitable if permissive commercial integration is desired |

2. Confirm compatibility with third-party dependencies and any copied/adapted code.
3. Select the licence explicitly.
