## Gate to move from NOT SUBMISSION READY

The manuscript can move to **SUBMISSION READY WITH AUTHOR SIGN-OFF** only after: (1) author fields/declarations are completed; (2) ethics and consent are institutionally resolved; (3) defensible Data Availability wording is approved; and (4) the exact current official MDPI template is applied, compiled successfully, and visually inspected.

A public release/DOI remains a separate release-readiness decision and is not required to be conflated with scientific manuscript readiness.
