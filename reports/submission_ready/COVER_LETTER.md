# Cover Letter — MDPI Information

Dear Editors of *Information*,

Please consider our manuscript, **“Beyond Predictive Accuracy: A Traceable Leakage- and Governance-Aware XAI Audit Protocol for Ordinal Employee Performance Prediction,”** for publication as an Original Research Article in *Information*.

Employee-performance prediction studies are often summarized by a single predictive score, although the scientific conclusion can change with feature-availability assumptions, model-selection objectives, ordinal error severity, probability quality, extreme-class behavior, subgroup support, target construction, and explanation identity. Our manuscript addresses this problem through a traceable audit protocol for ordinal HR prediction rather than by proposing a new prediction algorithm.

The main contribution is the operational integration of information-policy controls, nested model selection, exactly-once held-out predictions, class-aware ordinal evaluation, exact prediction–explanation identity, cross-fitted calibration, explanation-stability and deletion diagnostics, subgroup/proxy boundaries, target-mapping sensitivity, partial cross-dataset protocol replication, and a versioned manuscript-to-evidence ledger. The results show why aggregate ordinal improvements should not be interpreted as sufficient evidence of extreme-class recognition or prospective validity.

We believe the manuscript fits *Information* because it addresses applied artificial intelligence, data analysis, explainability, reproducible/traceable evaluation practice, and governance-relevant information-system methodology. The novelty claim is deliberately bounded: no individual audit component is presented as novel in isolation, and we do not make a world-first, fairness-certification, causal, prospective-validation, or deployment-readiness claim.
