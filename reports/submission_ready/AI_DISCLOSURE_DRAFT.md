# AI-Assisted Technology Disclosure Draft

**Status:** AUTHOR SIGN-OFF REQUIRED before submission.

MDPI's current policy requires transparency when generative AI is used beyond ordinary grammar/spellchecking or superficial text editing. Such use should be described in the Methods and acknowledged with the tool and purpose; AI tools cannot be authors and the authors remain responsible for the work.

## Proposed Methods wording

`AI-assisted tools were used during software-development support and manuscript drafting/revision. Generated suggestions were not treated as scientific evidence or as substitutes for model execution. Manuscript-facing numerical claims were checked against versioned aggregate evidence, source selectors, and deterministic validation procedures. The scientific analyses, reported results, interpretations, citations, and final submitted content remain the responsibility of the authors.`

## Proposed Acknowledgments wording

`During preparation of this manuscript, the authors used OpenAI ChatGPT and Codex for code-assistance and manuscript drafting/revision support. The authors reviewed and validated the resulting material and take full responsibility for the content of the publication.`

## Boundaries

- Do not list ChatGPT, Codex, OpenAI, or any other AI tool as an author.
- Do not imply that AI-generated text or suggestions constituted independent scientific evidence.
- Do not state that AI autonomously generated or altered original study data.
- Final wording must be approved by both authors and should reflect the actual tools/versions and uses at the time of submission.

Confirm the final product/version information requested by the submission system and approve the disclosure before it is copied into the journal manuscript.
