# ESWA journal requirements

Checked: **2026-09-09**. Journal: **Expert Systems with Applications**, ISSN 0957-4174. This is a preparation audit, not a submission receipt or author approval.

The official ScienceDirect Guide for Authors initially returned HTTP 403 to the web retrieval tool, but its complete live page was then read successfully in the in-app browser. Its public submission link and the journal's publication-options page were also opened. The Editorial Manager landing page identified the correct journal; authenticated submission fields and the generated submission PDF were not inspected. Recheck them on the actual submission day.

## Important corrections to the planning assumptions

- The current journal limit is **1–7 keywords**; this project retains the user's stricter **maximum six**.
- The current reference specification is **APA seventh edition**, with author–year citations and an alphabetical/chronological bibliography. Numeric citations or merely selecting a generic Elsevier numbered style do not satisfy this specification.
- The current guide says highlights and CRediT are **encouraged**. They remain required deliverables under this user's preparation plan.
- Review is **double anonymized**, confirmed in two sections of the current guide. Both the main manuscript and supplement must be anonymous, with a separate identifying title page.
- The data rule is **Option C**: deposit and cite/link data, or explain why sharing is not possible. The availability statement is required at submission.
- The current guide does not promise unrestricted initial formatting under “Your Paper Your Way.” Editable source is requested; a PDF alone is not a source file.

## Verified template and portal locations

The guide's template link points to [Elsevier CAS templates](https://assets.ctfassets.net/o78em1y1w4i4/5uFmLZJTPDMAUjFnHRpjj8/6f19a979146eb93263763d87a894ab0d/els-cas-templates.zip). The publisher's general LaTeX page also supplies the [official elsarticle package](https://assets.ctfassets.net/o78em1y1w4i4/4MpsJHO0MOJ2xZuwGTAbOZ/7bc64af36477c5d6cfce335a1f872363/elsarticle.zip). The latter supports the user's elsarticle preference; neither package's default bibliography is a substitute for checking the journal's APA requirement. Do not use the CRC-specific templates without an applicable journal instruction.

The guide points to [ESWA Editorial Manager][EM]. Exact portal article-type labels, required classification/reviewer fields, accepted archive organization, file visibility and the final subscription selection remain **unverified behind login**. No submission ID exists.
