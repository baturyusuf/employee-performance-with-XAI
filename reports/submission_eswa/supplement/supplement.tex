support}\label{table-s6.-subgroup-gap-uncertainty-and-support}

studentized maximum absolute bootstrap deviation over all estimable
prespecified P3 attribute, support-threshold, and metric gap cells.
Eligibility is fixed before resampling; fitted-model variability is
excluded. These diagnostics do not certify fairness.

\section{Table S8. Bounded literature
comparison}\label{table-s8.-bounded-literature-comparison}

\subsection{Li et al.~(2021)}\label{li-et-al.-2021}

\textbf{Problem and evidence:} Employee-performance classification;
Three linked internal HR tables from a publishing organization.
\textbf{Method and XAI:} Logistic regression; decision tree; naive
Bayes; Permutation importance reported. \textbf{Boundary relative to
this study:} Organization-specific comparison; present work joins
ordinal failure analysis to an explicit evidence contract. Official
proceedings PDF; no claim of an exhaustive absence audit.

\subsection{Adeniyi et al.~(2022)}\label{adeniyi-et-al.-2022}

\textbf{Problem and evidence:} Employee-performance classification;
Public HR table with 311 rows and 36 attributes. \textbf{Method and
XAI:} PCA; ANN; RF; DT; reported 80:20 split;
not\_reported\_in\_accessible\_source. \textbf{Boundary relative to this
study:} Present work separately audits target aliases, target mapping
and training-only evaluation. Publisher full text. Existing title
retained over conflicting Crossref deposit.

\subsection{Putri and Sitohang (2026)}\label{putri-and-sitohang-2026}

\textbf{Problem and evidence:} Employee-performance classification; INX
employee data. \textbf{Method and XAI:} Random Forest; multiple
train/test proportions; Feature importance. \textbf{Boundary relative to
this study:} Present work separates nested candidate selection from
final evaluation and audits metric-specific extreme-class failures.
Publisher PDF methods/results; non-reporting is not proof of absence.

\subsection{Löfström et al.~(2024)}\label{luxf6fstruxf6m-et-al.-2024}

\textbf{Problem and evidence:} Reliable local explanations with
uncertainty; 25 benchmark datasets. \textbf{Method and XAI:} Calibrated
Explanations based on Venn-Abers; Conditional feature explanations and
counterfactuals. \textbf{Boundary relative to this study:} A new
explanation method; present study audits existing exact-model SHAP and a
declared probability-calibration path. Publisher abstract and metadata;
do not infer unseen evaluation controls.

\subsection{Sepúlveda et al.~(2025)}\label{sepuxfalveda-et-al.-2025}

\textbf{Problem and evidence:} Stability of local interpretability;
Synthetic and real anomaly-detection data. \textbf{Method and XAI:}
Top-rank-weighted stability measure; SHAP-based feature-ranking
consistency under small changes. \textbf{Boundary relative to this
study:} Local perturbation stability differs from
fold/seed/training-resample comparisons and model-level deletion in the
present audit. Publisher abstract and section excerpts.

\subsection{Manafi Varkiani et
al.~(2025)}\label{manafi-varkiani-et-al.-2025}

\textbf{Problem and evidence:} Employee attrition and contextual
explanation; Italian financial-company employee records. \textbf{Method
and XAI:} Machine-learning case study; Random Forest explanation; SHAP
contribution magnitude and direction. \textbf{Boundary relative to this
study:} Strong HR-XAI precedent; different outcome and organization,
without evidence of the present shared audit contract in accessible
material. Publisher abstract and available methods excerpts.

\subsection{Rass and Dallinger (2026)}\label{rass-and-dallinger-2026}

\textbf{Problem and evidence:} Detecting unwanted patterns in training
data; Real-life and synthetic datasets. \textbf{Method and XAI:}
Prespecified rules; fuzzy reasoning; regression significance tests;
Rule-based explainability. \textbf{Boundary relative to this study:}
Proposes data-testing machinery; present study evaluates how multiple
system-audit views constrain claims on fixed HR evidence. Publisher
abstract and metadata; final publication year 2026 despite DOI
containing 2025.

\subsection{Abusitta et al.~(2024)}\label{abusitta-et-al.-2024}

\textbf{Problem and evidence:} Taxonomy and limitations of XAI;
Literature survey. \textbf{Method and XAI:} Review and comparative
taxonomy; Central topic. \textbf{Boundary relative to this study:}
General XAI guidance; present paper must show a concrete operational
audit rather than treat a component list as novelty. Publisher abstract
and section excerpts; not a benchmark competitor.

\subsection{Kapoor et al.~(2024),
REFORMS}\label{kapoor-et-al.-2024-reforms}

\textbf{Problem and evidence:} Reporting and reproducibility of ML-based
science; Consensus and methodological literature. \textbf{Method and
XAI:} Checklist and guidelines; Reporting context. \textbf{Boundary
relative to this study:} Important prior protocol. Present contribution
should be the executable linkage plus empirical interactions among audit
findings, not the invention of transparent evaluation. Author-maintained
project and registered Science Advances metadata.

\subsection{Bar-Gil et al.~(2024)}\label{bar-gil-et-al.-2024}

\textbf{Problem and evidence:} Embedding AI ethics in HR analytics; Two
organizational HR case studies. \textbf{Method and XAI:} Comparative
case-study analysis; Organizational governance context. \textbf{Boundary
relative to this study:} Shows why technical diagnostics cannot settle
organizational governance or employee consequences. Publisher abstract
and full-text excerpts.

\subsection{Lones (2024)}\label{lones-2024}

\textbf{Problem and evidence:} Avoiding common ML practice errors;
Tutorial and examples. \textbf{Method and XAI:} Practical methodological
guidance; Interpretation guidance. \textbf{Boundary relative to this
study:} Broad practical precedent; present study instantiates connected
checks in an ordinal HR setting. Publisher tutorial identity and
abstract; individual empirical comparisons are not inferred.

\subsection{Present study}\label{present-study}

\textbf{Problem and evidence:} Auditing ordinal employee-performance
prediction; INX; partial HRDataset\_v14 protocol replication.
\textbf{Method and XAI:} Nested benchmark; objective/policy
sensitivities; exact evidence identities; Exact-model grouped SHAP;
stability; model-level deletion. \textbf{Boundary relative to this
study:} Integration links conflicting findings to the evaluated system;
no new predictor or deployment claim. Frozen claim boundary; not an
additional experimental result.
