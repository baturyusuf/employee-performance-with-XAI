This workspace retargets the approved Round 2 scientific claims to *Expert Systems with Applications*. It does not modify the historical MDPI package, rerun an experiment, distribute employee-level data, choose a software licence, create a release, or submit the article.

The anonymous manuscript and scientific supplement are author-review drafts. Submission remains blocked by the open decisions in `declarations/AUTHOR_DECISIONS.md`, including ethics and consent determinations, complete author metadata, funding, competing interests, CRediT approval, AI-use attestation, data rights, software licence, repository-history strategy, and final author approval.
