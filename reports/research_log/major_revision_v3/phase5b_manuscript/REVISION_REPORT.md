# Revision Report

## Outcome

The manuscript was rewritten from title through declarations using the approved Phase 5A boundary as the only admissible claim set. The revised title is:

> Beyond Predictive Accuracy: A Reproducible Leakage- and Governance-Aware XAI Audit Protocol for Ordinal Employee Performance Prediction

The scientific center is now the shared evidence contract across feature availability, nested evaluation, ordinal metrics, exact-fold SHAP, stability/faithfulness, calibration, subgroup/proxy diagnostics, replication, and manuscript-number provenance.

## Removed material

The revision removes obsolete LLM, specialist-agent, chatbot, guardrail, 80-case explanation-run, readiness-dashboard, and heuristic recourse narratives from the scientific manuscript. It also removes the stale historical tag as a claimed final evidence identity. Those artifacts may remain in repository history for audit, but they are not Phase 5B manuscript evidence.

## Added evidence

- P0–P5 feature-availability contract and the cross-sectional estimand boundary.
- Nine-system exactly-once OOF benchmark with classification, ordinal, and probability metrics.
- Five fixed repeated nested-CV designs with ranking stability summaries.
- Fixed versus policy-specific-retuned comparisons.
- Exact prediction–fold–model–SHAP identity, stability, and deletion behavior.
- Extended calibration, subgroup support, proxy-use, and reconstruction diagnostics.
- HRDataset_v14 independent mapped-target protocol replication.
- Aggregate data-quality audit and source-rights boundary.
- Explicit unresolved author, ethics, licensing, history, release, and archival fields.
