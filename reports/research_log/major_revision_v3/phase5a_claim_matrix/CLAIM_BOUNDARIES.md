# Claim Boundaries

These prohibitions apply to every later abstract, result, discussion, conclusion, caption, response-to-reviewers statement, and repository/release description.

- The study proves causal determinants of employee performance.
- The evaluated systems are leakage-free.
- Retained features were verified as available before a real evaluation decision.
- The evidence establishes prospective or deployment validity.
- A universally best model or information policy was identified.
- SHAP values identify causal, actionable, or employee-level prescriptions.
- The subgroup analysis proves fairness or discrimination.
- Department reconstructability proves department use by the performance model.
- HRDataset_v14 is a locked-model external validation of the INX system.
- The two target mappings or organizational ratings are construct-equivalent.
- The literature review establishes a world-first or exhaustive novelty claim.
- Public source presence proves source authenticity or redistribution rights.
- Raw employee-level data are approved for public redistribution.
- Ethics approval, exemption, consent waiver, funding, conflicts, or author roles are resolved.
- The repository is open source despite the absence of a software license.
- A final tag, GitHub release, archival record, or DOI already exists.

No `not_reported`, missing, unsupported, pending, or unverified value may be converted to `no`, zero, approved, exempt, licensed, authentic, released, or resolved.
