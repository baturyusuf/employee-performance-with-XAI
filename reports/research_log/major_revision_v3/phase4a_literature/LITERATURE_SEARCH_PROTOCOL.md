# Literature Search and Verification Protocol

Retrieval cutoff: `2026-09-07`

## Objective

Identify close employee-performance/HR-XAI studies and the methodological sources needed to evaluate leakage, nested selection, calibration, explanation validity, responsible HR inference, and reproducible artifact lineage. The target is a bounded positioning set, not an exhaustive meta-analysis.

## Query families

- exact INX Future Inc name and acquisition filename
- employee performance prediction and machine learning
- HR analytics with XAI or SHAP
- HRDataset_v14 employee performance
- leakage-aware model evaluation and nested cross-validation
- explanation robustness, sensitivity, faithfulness, and SHAP stability
- probability calibration and calibration evaluation
- responsible algorithmic HR decision-making
- model reporting and reproducible machine-learning workflows

## Source-authority order

- publisher article or proceedings page
- publisher full text
- Crossref registered metadata
- official PMLR, JMLR, or NeurIPS proceedings
- authoritative open repository when no publisher record exists

Discovery results from general search, GitHub, resumes, portfolios, ResearchGate, and index aggregators were used only to locate primary records. A work entered the positioning set only after its identity was checked against a publisher/proceedings record, Crossref, or an authoritative repository. No paid API was called.

## Selection and missing-evidence rules

Include 15 to 25 works that are directly empirical, use the same or a closely analogous HR dataset, or supply a method/governance requirement used by the shared evidence contract. Do not pad with generic AI papers.

Assess methods from publisher full text when accessible; otherwise use the publisher abstract/metadata and mark unobserved design fields not_reported rather than infer their absence.

A DOI is registered only when Crossref returned the work on the retrieval date. Publisher-asserted but unresolved identifiers remain explicitly qualified and must not be described as registry-verified.

The 2019 IJCSE INX article is retained because the publisher record and PDF are direct prior-art evidence. Its printed DOI did not resolve in Crossref on the cutoff date, so downstream citation work must use the verified publisher URL and retain the unresolved-DOI caveat unless the identifier is later independently resolved.

## Operational positioning fields

- `employee_performance`: The modeled or substantively studied outcome is individual employee job performance; attrition-only work is not counted as employee performance.
- `same_or_similar_dataset`: The work uses the exact 1,200-row INX dataset, exact HRDataset_v14, or another employee-level HR prediction dataset close enough to inform design comparison.
- `leakage_policy`: An explicit prediction-time feature-availability or leakage-exclusion policy is stated beyond merely removing the target column.
- `nested_cv`: All data-dependent model or hyperparameter selection is nested inside training partitions used for performance estimation.
- `calibration`: Predicted probabilities are explicitly calibrated or evaluated as calibrated; reporting log loss alone does not qualify.
- `exact_fold_explanations`: Each held-out explanation is generated by the exact model fitted without that held-out observation and model/fold identity is retained.
- `shap_stability`: SHAP stability is quantitatively evaluated under repeated seeds, samples, resamples, folds, or controlled input perturbations; a single SHAP plot does not qualify.
- `subgroup_analysis`: Predictive performance or errors are compared across supported demographic or organizational groups; discussion of fairness alone does not qualify.
- `proxy_reconstruction`: The ability to reconstruct a protected or organizational attribute from retained predictors is explicitly tested.
- `second_dataset`: The empirical method is evaluated on a genuinely distinct dataset, not only multiple tables from one organization or multiple train/test splits.
- `artifact_provenance`: Machine-verifiable lineage binds reported outputs to source data, code/model/fold identity, environment, and immutable hashes; a data or code URL alone is insufficient.

Statuses are `yes`, `no`, `partial`, `not_reported`, and `not_applicable`. `partial` records a related but narrower mechanism. `not_applicable` is used when a non-empirical method/governance paper cannot fairly be judged as an application study.

## Search result boundary

The selected set contains four exact-INX studies (three primary uses and one secondary-dataset use), one exact-HRDataset_v14 study, four other close HR empirical/XAI studies, four HR governance/review sources, five XAI-validity sources, five evaluation/leakage/calibration sources, and two reporting/reproducibility sources. The exclusion log preserves close candidates that were displaced by the 25-work cap or could not be verified deeply enough.
