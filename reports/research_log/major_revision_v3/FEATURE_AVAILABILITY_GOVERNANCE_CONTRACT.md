## Prediction scenario

The analytical target is the recorded organisational PerformanceRating, evaluated as if prediction occurred before completion of the corresponding performance-evaluation decision, using only feature families permitted by each prespecified information policy.

**Evidence status:** `conceptual_estimand_only_no_observed_feature_or_decision_timestamps`

**Supported interpretation:** Cross-sectional leakage- and governance-risk sensitivity under explicit assumptions about feature availability.

**Prohibited interpretation:** The dataset does not establish that retained features were actually observed before a real evaluation decision, that all leakage was eliminated, or that the models are prospectively deployable.

## Mandatory limitations

- The INX file has no feature-observation timestamps, evaluation-period timestamps, or decision-completion timestamp.
- The workbook provides a partial seven-field codebook and does not establish complete semantic or source authority.
- P4 is a prospective-plausibility sensitivity, not verified prospective deployment evidence.
- P5 removes declared strong organisational channels but cannot prove absence of residual proxy information.
- No policy supports the claim that all leakage was eliminated.

This contract is an additive v3 scientific side input. It does not modify or relabel canonical v2 evidence.
