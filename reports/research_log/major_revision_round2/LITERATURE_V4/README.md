# Round 2 Core-Method Literature and Bibliography

Status: **source-verified additive preapproval package**

Retrieval date: `2026-09-08`

This package adds nine method references needed for self-contained reporting of the ordinal models, ordinal/probabilistic evaluation, and the three tree ensembles. It does not modify the historical Phase 4A package, `manuscript/mdpi_information/references.bib`, or manuscript prose.

Metadata were verified against Oxford Academic, Springer, World Scientific, the APA DOI record with PubMed corroboration, the American Meteorological Society, Taylor & Francis, ACM proceedings, and official NeurIPS proceedings. The official LightGBM proceedings record has no DOI, so none is invented.

## Files

- `CORE_METHOD_REFERENCES.csv`: machine-readable metadata, method coverage, verification basis, intended use, and claim boundary.
- `VERIFIED_CORE_METHOD_BIBLIOGRAPHY.md`: readable verified citations.
- `BIBTEX_CANDIDATES.bib`: additive entries proposed for integration only after claim-digest approval.
- `NOVELTY_BOUNDARY.md`: fixed integration claim and prohibited novelty wording.
- `provenance_receipt.json`: retrieval and scope receipt.
- `manifest.json`: closed-world byte inventory.

The former 25-work positioning cap is not applied where it would prevent complete citation of models and evaluation methods. This is a targeted bibliography supplement, not a new exhaustive or systematic review.
