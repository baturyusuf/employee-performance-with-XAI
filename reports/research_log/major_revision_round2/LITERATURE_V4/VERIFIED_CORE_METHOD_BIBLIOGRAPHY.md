# Verified Core-Method Bibliography

Retrieval date: **2026-09-08**

This list supplements, rather than replaces or edits, the frozen Phase 4A positioning package. Metadata were checked against publisher pages or official proceedings records. It is a preapproval bibliography source for later manuscript integration.

1. McCullagh, P. (1980). Regression Models for Ordinal Data. *Journal of the Royal Statistical Society Series B: Methodological*, 42(2), 109–127. DOI: [`10.1111/j.2517-6161.1980.tb01109.x`](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.2517-6161.1980.tb01109.x). Role: proportional-odds and cumulative-link foundation.
2. Frank, E., & Hall, M. (2001). A Simple Approach to Ordinal Classification. In *Machine Learning: ECML 2001*, LNCS 2167, 145–156. DOI: [`10.1007/3-540-44795-4_13`](https://doi.org/10.1007/3-540-44795-4_13). Role: binary-threshold ordinal reduction.
3. Cardoso, J. S., & Sousa, R. (2011). Measuring the Performance of Ordinal Classification. *International Journal of Pattern Recognition and Artificial Intelligence*, 25(8), 1173–1195. DOI: [`10.1142/S0218001411009093`](https://doi.org/10.1142/S0218001411009093). Role: ordinal-aware evaluation.
4. Cohen, J. (1968). Weighted Kappa: Nominal Scale Agreement Provision for Scaled Disagreement or Partial Credit. *Psychological Bulletin*, 70(4), 213–220. DOI: [`10.1037/h0026256`](https://doi.org/10.1037/h0026256). Role: weighted agreement/QWK foundation.
5. Epstein, E. S. (1969). A Scoring System for Probability Forecasts of Ranked Categories. *Journal of Applied Meteorology*, 8(6), 985–987. DOI: [`10.1175/1520-0450(1969)008<0985:ASSFPF>2.0.CO;2`](https://doi.org/10.1175/1520-0450(1969)008%3C0985:ASSFPF%3E2.0.CO;2). Role: ranked probability score.
6. Gneiting, T., & Raftery, A. E. (2007). Strictly Proper Scoring Rules, Prediction, and Estimation. *Journal of the American Statistical Association*, 102(477), 359–378. DOI: [`10.1198/016214506000001437`](https://doi.org/10.1198/016214506000001437). Role: proper probabilistic evaluation.
7. Breiman, L. (2001). Random Forests. *Machine Learning*, 45, 5–32. DOI: [`10.1023/A:1010933404324`](https://doi.org/10.1023/A:1010933404324). Role: Random Forest algorithm.
8. Chen, T., & Guestrin, C. (2016). XGBoost: A Scalable Tree Boosting System. In *Proceedings of the 22nd ACM SIGKDD International Conference on Knowledge Discovery and Data Mining*, 785–794. DOI: [`10.1145/2939672.2939785`](https://doi.org/10.1145/2939672.2939785). Role: XGBoost algorithm.
9. Ke, G., Meng, Q., Finley, T., Wang, T., Chen, W., Ma, W., Ye, Q., & Liu, T.-Y. (2017). LightGBM: A Highly Efficient Gradient Boosting Decision Tree. *Advances in Neural Information Processing Systems*, 30, 3146–3154. Official proceedings: [NeurIPS](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper/2017/hash/6449f44a102fde848669bdd9eb6b76fa-Abstract.html). No DOI is assigned in the official record.

## Use boundary

These sources establish method provenance and evaluation rationale. They do not validate this study's data, policy choices, folds, candidate grids, target construction, or conclusions. QWK, MAE, RPS, macro-F1, and class-specific performance answer different questions; no cited source turns them into a universal single ranking criterion.
