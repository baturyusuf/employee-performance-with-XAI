# Round 2 Novelty Boundary

The contribution is not the novelty of any single component, but their operational integration into a traceable evaluation contract.

The component methods—nested evaluation, proportional-odds modelling, cumulative-threshold ordinal classification, Random Forest, XGBoost, LightGBM, probability calibration, proper scoring, SHAP, subgroup diagnostics, and provenance recording—have prior literature. The study contribution is the operational link between explicit information-access policies, training-only nested selection and calibration, exact held-out prediction/explanation identity, stability and faithfulness checks, bounded subgroup/proxy interpretation, independent protocol replication, and source-bound manuscript claims.

Permitted wording is limited to this integration and to descriptive comparisons within the source-verified evidence set. The package does not support “world first,” “first ever,” an exhaustive-review claim, novelty of an individual algorithm or metric, elimination of all leakage/proxy information, fairness certification, causal interpretation, or deployment readiness.
